Come see what we're building.

Ballcrank Arcade sits on 38 acres off Reems Creek Road in Weaverville. On Saturday, October 24, we're opening it up to the community for an afternoon of live music, local food, shopping, free fitness classes and activities for kids. It's your first look at what Ballcrank is becoming, and it's free.

What's happening

  • Pickleball open play + clinics

    Get on the court, or learn the game from local players.

  • Live music

    Local musicians all afternoon.

  • Local vendors

    Shop from businesses based here in Western North Carolina.

  • Food trucks

    Come hungry.

  • Local farmers

    Meet the people who grow your food.

  • Artists and makers

    Handmade work from local artists.

  • Free fitness classes

    Try a class with local instructors.

  • Kids' activities

    Dance, movement and plenty of room to run.

  • Wellness businesses

    Meet massage therapists, physical therapists and movement practitioners.

  • Property tours

    See the 38 acres and hear what's planned.
